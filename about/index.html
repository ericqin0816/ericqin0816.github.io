Hey, I’m Eric.

I’m a student at Seven Lakes High School in Katy, Texas. I spend a lot of my time climbing, swimming, and working on projects that start with something I’m curious about.

Some of those projects are things I use myself, like a tool for reviewing my climbing videos. Others let me follow a question a little further: how a neural network learns a sequence, where a steering model goes wrong, or when solar panels start to make financial sense.
