ClimbIQ

Creator & Engineer

A local-first speed-climbing video-analysis platform. Race footage becomes candidate events and splits, with frame review and correction when the evidence is ambiguous.

Case study ↗GitHub ↗
Engineering notes +

React, TypeScript, Vite, MediaPipe Pose, Canvas, Web Audio, and WebAssembly. Video stays on-device; attempts save locally and can be compared and exported.

Evaluated across 11 source recordings and 30 controlled variants, with 126 frame seek/pixel checks and 230+ noisy-state automated trials. Testing covers compression, resizing, exposure, frame-rate changes, missing audio, and trimming.

MediaPipe is an existing model I integrated. General timing accuracy has not been established.

Council Check-in

Workflow automation

A scan-to-sheet attendance system built for Student Council meetings. Student IDs become check-ins, with member lookup and date matching handled automatically.

How it works +

A USB barcode scanner and Chromebook work with Google Sheets and Apps Script. Members are rows; meeting dates are columns.

Handled duplicate scans, unknown IDs, incorrect row creation, changing meeting-date columns, and cell-indexing issues.

Solar + Battery Economics

Research in development

Modeling when residential solar and battery storage become economically worthwhile for a Texas homeowner.

Research direction +

The model considers home-energy inputs, solar and battery costs, electricity rates, and system performance to explore payback and a break-even threshold.

Scenario comparisons, rate ranges, and sensitivity analysis are directions for the developing model. No results are claimed yet.
